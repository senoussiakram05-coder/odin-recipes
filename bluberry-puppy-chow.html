bleuberry puppy chow

This blueberry puppy chow gives the classic snack a fruity twist by coating fresh blueberries in a chocolate-and-peanut-butter mixture and dusting them with confectioners’ sugar. Ready in about 30 minutes, it’s a fun no-bake snack for summer.

Ingrediens:

  • 1/2 cup semisweet chocolate chips.
  • 2 tablespoons creamy peanut butter
  • 1 teaspoon coconut oil
  • 2 cups fresh blueberries
  • 1 cup confectioners sugar

Steps:

  1. In a large bowl, melt the chocolate chips, peanut butter, and coconut oil in the microwave for about 2 minutes in 30-second intervals, stirring each time until smooth.
  2. Add blueberries to the bowl, gently tossing them until completely coated
  3. In batches, sprinkle the confectioners' sugar over the berries and gently toss until completely coated.
  4. Pour them out onto a lined baking sheet and let cool completely, about 15 minutes.
  5. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to a week.
